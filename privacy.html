Privacy policy

Front Pew privacy policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026.

Your library lives on your device. Sermons, notes, handwriting and photos are stored on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account and we run no server that receives them. We never see them.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics. Front Pew collects no data about you or how you use it.

Links and feeds. When you paste a link or follow a channel, the app fetches its public title, picture and feed from that site (for example YouTube). Those sites see the request as they would any visit.

Video. Sermons play in YouTube’s own embedded player in privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube may set cookies once you press play; YouTube’s privacy policy applies to the video. Front Pew uses YouTube API Services to show video: by watching you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s Privacy Policy applies to it.

Searching for a sermon. The words you type in “Search for a sermon” are sent to YouTube (Google, through the YouTube Data API) to find videos, and to the public feeds of churches you follow. Nothing else goes with them, and we never see them.

Week packs from a leader. A leader’s week pack (the sermon, questions, readings and any fill-in-the-blank outline) travels inside the link or QR code itself. Opening it sends nothing to us; on the web page the pack sits after the # in the address, which browsers never send to a server, and anything you type there stays on your phone.

Verses. Tapping a verse asks bible-api.com for that reference. Only the reference is sent.

AI. Study guides and answers are made on your device, by Apple’s on-device model where your iPhone or iPad has one. Nothing is sent to an AI company.

Reminders. Notifications are created on your device.

Deleting. Delete a sermon and it is gone. Delete the app and everything goes with it. You can export your library first.

Children. Front Pew does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact. Email Front Pew support.
